# UNWRITTEN: Era I canon

Structured summary of the owner's latest supplied design. This is design canon,
not an implementation claim. It supersedes earlier five-role/gacha brainstorms.

## Identity and world

Astraea is the world; Asterra is a kingdom within it. Persistent multiplayer fantasy
RPG: **The world remembers the players.** Tragedy, comedy, friendship, mystery,
politics, romance, horror, war and exploration all belong here. Not uniformly grimdark.

Regions: Asterra, grasslands, forests, goblin settlements, ancient ruins, Ashen Peaks,
Ancient Forest, western coast, underwater regions, forgotten valleys, Forbidden
Forest, Tower of Silence, Hell, Heaven, Nameless Desert, Sunken Realm and hidden
dimensions. Destruction persists outside protected areas. Resources regenerate by
rarity. NPC refugees and gradual reconstruction must be visible. Players may
contribute gold or materials; participation is optional.

## Character and combat

First login: alone in a white void, no HUD/inventory/other players. An unidentified
entity presents seven physical weapons. This is AION; do not reveal his identity
until the finale. SYSTEM AWAKENED, ROLE GRANTED, randomized Passive, then the white
world breaks and the player falls into Astraea.

| Weapon | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sword | Warrior |
| Bow | Archer |
| Staff | Mage |
| Shield | Tanker |
| Sacred Tome | Healer |
| Daggers | Rogue |
| Gauntlets | Fighter |

Role changes only through Academy. Style is a separate specialization, changeable
outside combat. Each Style equips exactly three active skills with evolution
paths. Awakening temporarily transforms these three skills, never a fourth slot.
Limited/Mythical/Legacy rarity is not automatically statistical superiority.

Initial Passives may be hidden as ???. Dragon Affinity, Heaven's Grace, Colossus
Blood, Beast Kinship and Unknown Authority can affect encounters. Hidden Fate
responds to reputation, heroism, contribution, story and championship status.
Hero Candidate is a possible Warrior Fate, not automatic on level-up.

Combat: basic/heavy attacks, block, parry, dodge/perfect dodge, counter, skills,
Awakening and weapon passives. Some AoE, ultimates and environmental attacks cannot
be dodged. Perfect dodge rewards depend on Role. Aura can cause fear, hesitation,
retreat or flight in weak monsters; elites resist and bosses react differently.

## Society

Adventurers' Guild ranks F/E/D/C/B/A/S. Guild is an institution, separate from
player clans. Clans have identity, emblems, territory, alliances and history.
Party encounters: Dullahan max 4, Caelum max 4, Nameless Knight solo. Raids support
world bosses, invasions, kingdom defense, Demon King and Thalassia.

Factions: Asterra, Adventurers' Guild, Merchant Guild, Church, Underworld, Goblin
Tribes, Demon Kingdom, Heaven, villages and dark factions. Reputation changes
hostility and quest access. Players can become King, Commander, Captain, Guard,
Minister or Guild Master. King is not admin; rebellion, assassination, loss of
support and replacement remain possible.

PvP legal only in designated contexts; towns and villages are safe. Illegal killing
produces red [PK] status, guards, bounties, NPC refusals, Underworld opportunities
and larger death penalties. Ordinary death does not drop the entire inventory.
Colosseum PvP does not grant PK. Weekly 1v1 winner holds the unique transferable
STRONGEST OF ASTERRA title. Previous champions persist in a hall/statue record.

Pet ranks: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic. The asset folder
pet_mythical retains the older roster label only. Pets can come from rescue,
merchants, quests, rare encounters, bosses and black markets. They have traits
and passives. Injured animal rescue may earn recognition much later.
Illegal Merchant appears through time/reputation/progression/world conditions;
contraband trades may affect crime and reputation.

Ecology tracks faction population: Goblin overhunting may cause migration,
abandoned settlements, stronger replacement tribes, revenge or invasion.
Rank S remains ecological calamity, not Colossus. World bosses may respawn.
Colossus outcomes follow the specific story rules below.

## Decem Fata: The Ten Fates

Ten Colossi is the popular name, not a species. Each existence can alter world laws
or history. I–VIII are discoverable in different orders. IX and X lead to the finale.

| Number | Existence | Canon |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| I | Vharos, The Sleeping Calamity | Ashen Peaks resource cavern. Mining/activity raises Disturbance. Failed awakenings return him to sleep. At 100 unsuccessful awakenings he attacks the nearest major kingdom. Failure means real destruction, refugees and reconstruction. Permanent death; each eligible participant gets Scale of Vharos ×1. |
| II | The Dullahan, Rider of the Last Night | Night wilderness signs: silence, absent monsters, fog, three bells, hoofbeats. Held head selects player. Mounted then dismounted fight. Defeat is not ordinary death; legacy items open The Man Who Lost His Name and possible Headless Rider Style. His name was erased. |
| III | Caelum, The Fallen Hero | A Love That Survived Eternity. Elara's ghost guides to a hidden grave. Intimate technical sword duel, then acceptance and reunion. Blade, armor, key and Hero's Journal rewards. Journal recalls the same white-void sword choice. |
| IV | Kael, The King Below | Friendly early hunter who genuinely likes humanity. THE FRACTURE precedes disappearance and Hell throne reveal. Same recognizable face in both forms. Highest damage earns DEMON KING SLAYER. Death releases him and creates a political power vacuum. |
| V | Orun, The God Built by Men | Secret dungeon chamber leads to Nameless Desert. Vertical climb, moving weakpoints, Core Chamber finale. Totem of Orun prevents one death then breaks. No respawn or repeat acquisition. |
| VI | Seraphiel, The Fallen Grace | Respected Royal Knight from Heaven. A Healer's Divine Recognition triggers envy. He transforms during an existing world-boss defense: both threats at once. Escape requires rediscovery, not immediate retry. Six damaged wings, broken halo. |
| VII | The Nameless Knight, The One Who Stood Alone | Qualified solo player enters Tower of Silence. Technical martial duel shifts to rooftop/exterior. First victor earns unique title. Knight asks the player's name and says Remember mine, but dies without saying it. Linked to Dullahan and erased early resistance against AION. |
| VIII | The Primordial Beasts | ONE Colossus in TWO existences: Thalassia and Verdant. Thalassia is Sunken Realm mother. Giant Verdant revives while she lives. Her death causes forest change, giant decay and emergence of The Last Father. Humanoid fast melee form loses fire weakness. Final seed grows into permanent Last Tree weeks later. |
| IX | Morrow, The Unwritten | Moving Black Door, solo entry. Anomaly takes zero damage and kills attackers instantly. Enter unarmed, unarmored, without combat items, carrying Cheese. He voluntarily follows. One active Morrow only; status UNRESOLVED. Comedy and mystery are essential. |
| X | AION, The Architect | Prologue entity. After I–VIII resolution and Morrow's discovery, he recalls players to White World and attempts WORLD RESET. Clean humanoid intimidation, not a giant monster. |

## Finale and epilogue

Phase I: AION removes Role, Style, Passive and equipment temporarily. Seven original
weapons return. Players take their original weapon and return to the basics.
Phase II: he mimics real historical player appearance, builds, three skills,
Passives and combat behavior recorded by World Chronicle.
Phase III: Architect form edits arena/System. Players lose; World Reset begins.
DELETE ENTITY fails against Morrow. True Morrow disrupts Authority because he was
never in AION's System, not simply because he has higher damage.

YOUR POWER IS YOUR OWN. Player kits return independently of AION. Factions helped
through Era I can join: Goblins, Demons, Heaven, Guild, clans, kingdom soldiers,
pets and NPCs. The server plus Morrow defeat AION. He recognizes what players became
and admits they never needed him. Morrow returns to his small form.

AUTHORITY: SELF. **No wipe and no actual world reset.** Houses, kingdoms, clans,
guilds, titles, governments, relationships, tournament records and Chronicle
persist. Barriers fall, revealing new continents and civilizations. Era II,
BEYOND THE CREATED WORLD, continues in this same world, built on Era I history.

## Quests and Chronicle

No traditional daily-quest treadmill. Main, Side, Hidden, World, Faction, Legacy,
Fate, Pet, Political and Discovery quests emerge from NPCs, exploration and action.
Some have no marker or log entry. Items, restraint, time, Passive, reputation,
earlier help, Champion/PK/King status, ghosts and secret chambers can be triggers.

Example: spare a Goblin child and receive Wooden Doll. Much later, around VIII
storyline progression, it reacts. The child is now a Chieftain and may create a
human–Goblin alliance based on that small early decision.

World Chronicle stores real player history: champions, Heroes, maximum bounties,
royal deaths/coronations, Vharos destruction/death, Kael's death, Guild elections,
Colossus outcomes, clan wars, alliances and World Tree planting. New players read
these events in an in-world library or monument.

Themes: freedom versus predetermined Fate, godhood and Authority, creation
surpassing its creator, erased history, and survival without a controller.
UNWRITTEN means first the player's future, then Morrow's unrecorded existence,
then a future without an Architect.
